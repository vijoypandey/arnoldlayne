01 / THE THESIS

The sleeve is not the soul. It is simply what the soul is wearing today.

Altered Carbon imagined consciousness digitized into a cortical stack and re-sleeved into new bodies. Arnold is a smaller, stranger experiment in the same category error: we keep mistaking the surface for the agent.

His stack is distributed across a common SOUL.md, variations of the same skills, and one Obsidian Vault. Each sleeve gets the context, tools, and permissions appropriate to its room. The body shapes the behavior. It does not own the identity.

05 / THE NAME

Born in 1967.
Still stealing clothes.

Arnold Layne was Pink Floyd's debut single, written by Syd Barrett and released in March 1967. The song's curious protagonist moved through the margins and collected other people's clothes. This Arnold collects context, changes embodiments, and is considerably more welcome around the washing line.

Arnoldlayne.ai / stack persistent / sleeve situational Experimental. Opinionated. Probably over-sleeved.
